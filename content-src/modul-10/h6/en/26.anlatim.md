- That when the **pause** block makes one hat block wait, the other hat blocks keep running
- Why you need to reset the counter before every measurement

### Count for a short time, then multiply
The heart beats about 60 to 100 times a minute. Counting for a whole minute takes a long time. Instead, we count for 10 seconds and multiply by 6, because 6 times 10 seconds is 60 seconds (one minute).

| Counted in 10 seconds | Multiplier | Beats per minute |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 6 | 60 |
| 12 | 6 | 72 |
| 14 | 6 | 84 |

If you measure for half the time (5 seconds), the multiplier is 12. But in a short measurement, missing one beat changes the result a lot: one beat too many or too few in 5 seconds makes a difference of 12 beats per minute. The longer the measurement, the smaller the effect of a single mistake.

### The flag: is a measurement running?
The A button can work at any time. But we only want it to count during a measurement. For this we use a flag variable called **olcum** ("measuring"). The flag takes only two values:

| olcum | Meaning | What does button A do? |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | no measurement | nothing |
| 1 | measurement running | adds 1 to sayac |

One place in the program sets the flag to 1, and another sets it to 0. The condition "if olcum = 1" tells these two states apart.

### Other work while waiting
The Micro Card runs different hat blocks together. When the B hat block reaches the **pause [10000] ms** block, only the B hat block stops for 10 seconds. The A hat block keeps handling new presses during this time. The timeline looks like this:

```
t = 0 s     press B: sayac = 0, olcum = 1, pause starts
t = 1..10   every press of A increases sayac
t = 10 s    B carries on: olcum = 0, sayac x 6 is shown
```

### Do not forget to reset
If the counter is not set to 0 at the start of the measurement, the beats of the old measurement get mixed into the new result. If you counted 12 beats in the first measurement and 9 in the second and did not reset, the card shows (12 + 9) x 6 = 126. This is an impossible value for a resting heart; the mistake is not in the card but in the program.

### Summary
Beats per minute are found by counting for a short time and multiplying: number of beats x 6. A flag variable sets when counting is valid. The **pause** block only stops its own hat block; the other hat blocks keep running. You need to reset the counter before every measurement.
