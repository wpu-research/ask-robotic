# MPU6050 accelerometer and gyroscope
slug: mpu6050
category: Input
summary: A three-axis accelerometer and gyroscope that talks over I2C. It measures tilt and rotation speed.

## Pins
- VCC: Power. Connects to 5V (needs at least about 3 V).
- GND: Ground.
- SDA: I2C data line. A4 on the Uno.
- SCL: I2C clock line. A5 on the Uno.

## How it works
The **MPU6050** is an I2C chip that contains a three-axis accelerometer and a three-axis gyroscope. The accelerometer measures gravity and acceleration in g, and the gyroscope measures rotation speed in °/s. The chip is asleep at power-up; it wakes up when you write 0 to register 0x6B. The measurements are in 14 bytes starting at 0x3B: acceleration X, Y, Z, temperature, gyroscope X, Y, Z (2 bytes each, high byte first, 16-bit signed).

In the default ranges, the raw value is divided by 16384 for acceleration (g) and by 131 for the gyroscope (°/s). Because a chip at rest measures gravity, the tilt angle is found with `atan2(ax, az)`. The accelerometer does not drift but is sensitive to vibration; the gyroscope is fast but builds up drift. The two are combined with a complementary filter.

**Advanced:** The temperature is `raw / 340 + 36.53` degrees. A real gyroscope shows an offset of a few °/s when at rest; the datasheet allows a tolerance of up to ±20 °/s for the initial offset. The lab chip is ideal, so it has no offset and its temperature is fixed at 25 °C.

## Wiring in the lab
| From | To |
|---|---|
| MPU6050 **VCC** | Uno **5V** |
| MPU6050 **GND** | Uno **GND** |
| MPU6050 **SDA** | Uno **A4** |
| MPU6050 **SCL** | Uno **A5** |

The properties panel has **İvme X, Y, Z** (Acceleration X, Y, Z, in g), **Jiroskop X, Y, Z** (Gyroscope X, Y, Z, in °/s) and **I2C adresi (AD0)** (I2C address). The values can also be changed while the program is running. If you choose address 0x69, you must also change the `MPU` constant in your code to 0x69.

## Typical values
- I2C address | 0x68 | 0x69 if AD0 is connected to 5V
- Chip lying flat | ax = 0, ay = 0, az = 1 g | Lab default
- Acceleration conversion | raw / 16384 | In the ±2 g range; 1 g = 16384
- Gyroscope conversion | raw / 131 | In the ±250 °/s range
- Temperature | 25 °C | Fixed for the lab chip
- Tilt example | ax = 0.5, az = 0.866 → 30° | pitch = atan2(ax, az)

## Common mistakes
- **Forgetting to write 0 to register 0x6B.** The chip stays asleep.
- **Writing ax / 16384.** Integer division gives 0; write 16384.0.
- **Combining the bytes as unsigned.** A negative value shows up as something like 65000; use int16_t.
- **Using it on the same bus as the RTC (DS3231).** Both are at address 0x68.
- **Trusting the angle of a moving chip.** The accelerometer cannot tell gravity apart from the chip's own acceleration.
