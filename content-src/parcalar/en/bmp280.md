# BMP280 pressure sensor
slug: bmp280
category: Sensor
summary: A sensor that reports air pressure and temperature over I2C. Altitude can be calculated from the pressure.

## Pins
- VCC: Supply lead. Connected to 5V in the lab.
- GND: Ground lead. Connected to GND.
- SDA: I2C data line. Goes to the Uno's A4 pin.
- SCL: I2C clock line. Goes to the Uno's A5 pin.

## How it works
The BMP280 measures air pressure. As you go higher there is less air above you, so the pressure drops; that is why **altitude** can be calculated from pressure. The sensor sits on the I2C bus at address **0x76** or **0x77**. It can share the same two wires (SDA and SCL) with other I2C devices that have different addresses.

In the `Adafruit_BMP280` library, `bmp.begin(0x76)` looks for the sensor at that address; the library's default address is 0x77. `readPressure()` returns the pressure in **Pa**, and `readTemperature()` returns the temperature in degrees Celsius. `readAltitude(P0)` calculates the altitude and expects the sea-level pressure P0 in **hPa**.

**Advanced:** The altitude formula `44330 × (1 − (P / P0) ^ (1 / 5.255))` is based on the standard atmosphere assumption. Because P0 changes with the weather, the altitude reading drifts too; near sea level, 1 hPa is about 8 m. Check the datasheet for the supply voltage of a real module.

## Wiring in the lab
| From | To |
|---|---|
| BMP280 **VCC** | Uno **5V** |
| BMP280 **GND** | Uno **GND** |
| BMP280 **SDA** | Uno **A4** |
| BMP280 **SCL** | Uno **A5** |

The properties panel has **Sıcaklık** (Temperature, -40 to 85 °C), **Basınç** (Pressure, 300 to 1100 hPa) and **I2C adresi** (I2C address, 0x76 or 0x77). Temperature and pressure can be changed while the program is running. The address in your code must match the address in the properties panel.

## Typical values
- Standard pressure at sea level | 1013.25 hPa | 101 325 Pa
- I2C address | 0x76 (default) | 0x77 can also be selected in the lab
- Pressure 954.5 hPa | ≈ 500 m | For P0 = 1013.25 hPa
- Pressure 898.75 hPa | ≈ 1000 m | For P0 = 1013.25 hPa
- 1 hPa change | ≈ 8 m | Near sea level

## Common mistakes
- **Forgetting the address.** Getting a "not found" result with `begin()` without a parameter while the sensor is at 0x76.
- **Mixing up Pa and hPa.** If you write `readAltitude(101325)`, the altitude comes out very wrong.
- **Swapping SDA and SCL.** The sensor is not found at all.
- **Not checking the result of `begin`.** Without a sensor, you read meaningless numbers.
- **Treating the altitude as exact.** When the weather changes, P0 changes and the altitude drifts even though you have not moved.
