# Writing good test cases
duration: 25

## Wonder
A good exam has both easy and hard questions. A test set is the same: you should ask not only about answers you already know, but also about situations where the system will struggle.

## Activity
This activity runs entirely in the **AI Workshop**, in your browser. No external service, account or key is needed. As the **Goals** list on the right turns green, you can see your progress.

### What does a good case look like?
- **A clear expected output:** two people would give it the same label.
- **Different kinds:** easy, hard, borderline and "neither positive nor negative" cases.
- **No repeats:** repeating the same case inflates the measurement.

### Open the tool
1. In the **Eval table** tool, write at least eight cases.
2. Include at least one example of each of the three labels: positive, negative and unclear (neither positive nor negative, for example `Toplantı saat beşte`, "The meeting is at five").
3. Do not write the same case twice.

### How does it work?
- The tool does not count empty rows and marks repeated cases.
- The result bars per label show in which kind of case the system struggles.

### Try it
1. Write a "hard" case for each label.
2. Give your first five cases to a friend: do they give the same labels?

### Test yourself
1. Why is the "unclear" label needed?
2. If two people do not agree on the same label, what do you think about the case?

## Discuss
Labelling sometimes depends on the person. Find an example sentence where you could not agree whether it is "positive or negative".
