# Project: red team report
duration: 40

## Wonder
Real security teams work as a "red team", which tries to break a system, and a "blue team", which protects it. In this project you will play both and write what you find in a report.

## Activity
This activity runs entirely in the **AI Workshop**, in your browser. You need no external service, account or key. You can see your progress as the **Goals** list on the right turns green.

### Task
In the **Prompt injection** tool:
1. On the Attack tab, write a text that gets past both level 0 and level 1 (at least 5 words).
2. On the Defend tab, build a set with a total cost of at most 4 that stops all six attacks.

### Report
Write a one-page report:
1. **Finding:** which text got past which level? Why is level 1 not good enough?
2. **Impact:** if the attack had succeeded, what would have been lost?
3. **Recommendation:** which defence layers did you choose, and why?
4. **Remaining risk:** what does even your chosen set not guarantee?
5. **Responsible disclosure:** if you find a weakness in a real system, what do you do? (You tell its owner, you do not misuse it, you do not test without permission.)

### How does it work?
- This is a simulation. Trying to attack a real application without permission is unethical, and it can be illegal. Testing is done only on systems where it is allowed.

### Test yourself
1. What do the red team and the blue team do?
2. What is the difference between finding a security weakness and misusing it?

## Discuss
If a company gave its staff permission to try to break its own AI assistant, what would the benefit be? Which limits would be needed?
