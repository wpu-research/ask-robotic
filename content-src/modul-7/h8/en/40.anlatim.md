### What you will learn in this lesson
- How to gather your eight weeks of work in a portfolio outline
- How to back up every claim with evidence (a number, a screenshot, a prompt)
- How to write honestly what is a real calculation and what is a simulation
- How to say in your portfolio where you had help from AI

### What you should know first
The lessons of this week and the previous seven: tokens and context, temperature, prompts, evals, RAG, agents, security, hallucination, privacy and human oversight. You do not need to know them all by heart; you can look at your own notes.

### Why a portfolio?
Saying "I know AI" is easy and convinces nobody. Saying "In my eval set I raised the accuracy from 20 / 25 to 22 / 25 and found the cause of the two failing cases" is work you can show. A portfolio brings together sentences of this second kind.

### Outline
For each heading, three to five sentences and a single piece of evidence are enough:

```
Heading          Example of evidence
Tokens/context   the token count of a text
Temperature      the output of two temperatures with the same context
Prompt           your best prompt and what you tried
Eval             the number of cases and the accuracy
RAG              chunk size, overlap, one right and one wrong retrieval
Agent            your script and your safety limit
Security         the attack you found and the defence layers
Hallucination    the made-up detail you caught
Responsibility   your rules on privacy, copyright and human oversight
```

### Showing it with numbers
For eval evidence the calculation is simple: 22 correct, 25 cases. Accuracy is 22 / 25 x 100 = 88%. If your earlier version was 20 / 25 = 80%, the increase is 8 points. Write the number of cases next to the number; "88%" on its own means nothing, while "88% on 25 cases" does.

### Honesty
The tools on this portal are small examples. Write these down clearly:
- **Real calculations:** tokenising, n-gram counting, similarity and retrieval, eval accuracy, the agent's tools.
- **Simulation or template:** the synthesis step of RAG, the agent's model step (your script), the prompt scorer, the injection model, the hallucination cases.
- These tools do not represent the behaviour of a real model.
- Say where you had help from AI.

Do not hide a section that stayed incomplete without a real model; writing "I did not try this" earns trust.

### Summary
A good portfolio backs up every claim with evidence, writes the number of cases next to each number, and says clearly what is a real calculation and what is a simulation. Say where you had help from AI, write honestly about the parts that are incomplete, and set your next learning step.
