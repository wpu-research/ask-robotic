# Project: engineering portfolio
duration: 40

## Wonder
Over eight weeks you built small tools and got to know real ideas. Now gather what you have learned in a short portfolio: instead of telling people "I know AI", you can show what you did and what you measured.

## Activity
This is a reading and writing lesson; there is no new activity tool. You can open the tools from earlier lessons again in the **AI Workshop** (the free gallery).

### Portfolio outline
For each of the headings below, write three to five sentences and show one piece of evidence (a screenshot, a number or a prompt).
1. **Tokens and context:** explain how many tokens a text takes up, and whether it fits in the window.
2. **Temperature:** compare a low and a high temperature with the same context.
3. **Prompt:** write your best prompt and why it is good. A checklist score is not enough evidence; write what you tried.
4. **Eval:** your test set, your accuracy and your most interesting failing case.
5. **RAG:** your chunk size and overlap settings; what did retrieval get right, and what did it get wrong?
6. **Agent:** your script and your safety limits.
7. **Security:** the attack you found and the defence layers you chose.
8. **Hallucination:** the made-up detail you caught and your method of checking.
9. **Responsibility:** your rules on privacy, copyright and human oversight.

### Principles of honesty
- Write clearly which things are real calculations and which are simulations. The tools on this portal are small examples; they do not represent the behaviour of real models.
- If you used help from AI, say where you used it.

### Test yourself
1. Which claim in your portfolio can you show with a number?
2. Which section did you have to leave incomplete without a real model?
3. What would you like to learn as your next step?

## Discuss
Write the three most important lessons you take away from the eight-week journey. Do you now see something you said in the first week differently?
