# Project: An announcement summariser prompt
duration: 40

## Wonder
The week's project: a summariser prompt you could really use. It has a role, a clear audience, and a written example, format and constraint. When someone else reads this prompt, they should be able to understand what it does and why.

## Activity
This activity runs entirely in the **AI Workshop**, in your browser. No external service, account or key is needed. As the **Goals** list on the right turns green, you can see your progress.

### Task
Write a complete prompt that summarises robot club announcements for 13-year-old students.

### Requirements
- A checklist score of at least 90.
- At least 40 words.
- The words "announcement" and "summary" must appear.
- All six parts: task, role, context, example, format, constraint.

### Steps
1. Write the draft.
2. Add the parts the checker says are missing.
3. Simplify the prompt: every sentence should do a job.
4. Write a short "design note": why did you choose this role, this example, this format?

### How does it work?
- A constraint in the prompt such as "do not add information that is not in the announcement" asks the model to stay faithful to the source. This gives no guarantee; in lesson 36 you will learn to catch made-up information.
- Give the prompt to a friend and see which sentence they get stuck on.

### Test yourself
1. Which sentence in your prompt could you take out without the quality of the answer dropping?
2. Why is saying a prompt is good without measuring it a weak claim?

## Discuss
Put the week's prompts side by side. Which parts do they have in common? Which one brought a surprising idea?
