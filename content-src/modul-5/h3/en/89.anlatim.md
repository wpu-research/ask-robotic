### Patterns of the difficult cases
On a straight line the pattern is around `00100`. Three cases leave a different pattern:

| Case | Pattern | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Sharp left bend | `11100` | The left side and the middle on the line |
| Sharp right bend | `00111` | The middle and the right side on the line |
| T or + junction | `11111` | All on the line |
| Gap or loss | `00000` | None on the line |

### A gap, or an overshot bend?
`00000` is the sign of two different events. What separates them is where the line was last seen. If the line disappeared while it was last in the middle, the robot was going straight; most probably there is a **gap**, and it goes straight. If the line disappeared while it was last on an edge sensor, the robot was turning and **overshot** the bend; it turns on the spot towards the direction the line was last seen in and searches for it. So if `sonYon == 0` it moves to the `BOSLUK` state, otherwise to `ARAMA`.

### Time limit
Going straight in a gap is limited by `BOSLUK_MS`, and turning during a search by `ARAMA_MS`. If the time runs out, the line really is lost and the robot stops (`DUR`). An unlimited search spins the robot forever if there is no line at all. Stopping is the safe default. The distance covered depends on the speed: if the speed increases, it travels further in the same time, so `BOSLUK_MS` must be rethought along with the speed.

### The state machine
Four states are enough:

```
IZLE   -> none sees it: if sonYon == 0, BOSLUK, otherwise ARAMA
BOSLUK -> line seen: IZLE; time up: ARAMA
ARAMA  -> line seen: IZLE; time up: DUR
DUR    -> motors off
```

### The junction decision
When the robot sees `11111`, it does not turn straight away: the sensor array is about 7 cm in front of the wheel axle, and a robot that turns at once turns before it is in the middle of the junction. There are three stages: first go forward briefly (so the axle reaches the junction), then turn on the spot in the chosen direction (finish when the middle sensor has left the old line and found the new one); if straight was decided, go straight briefly. The decision is fixed: −1 left, 0 straight, +1 right.

### Summary
Sharp bends, junctions and gaps are recognised by their five-bit patterns. When `00000` appears, the last direction tells a gap from an overshot bend. The state machine works with the states `IZLE`, `BOSLUK`, `ARAMA` and `DUR`; at a junction it first goes forward, then turns. Every search has a limited time; when the time runs out, the robot stops.
