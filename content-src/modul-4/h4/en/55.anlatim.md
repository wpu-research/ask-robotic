### The panel's three layers
You can picture the inside of a fire panel like this:

```
INPUT         DECISION           OUTPUT
Gas (A3)   -> seviyeBul -> gaz   \
Flame (A1) -> seviyeBul -> alev   > enKotu -> genel -> LED, buzzer, screen
Heat (A2)  -> seviyeBul -> isi   /
                           gaz -> relay (fan)
```

The same `seviyeBul` function is called with different thresholds for each sensor. Adding a new sensor takes one line.

### Thresholds
| Sensor | Quantity | UYARI on / off | TEHLIKE on / off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gas | reading, multiple of the baseline | baseline × 1.5 / × 1.25 | baseline × 2.5 / × 2 |
| Flame | intensity | 300 / 250 | 600 / 500 |
| Heat | °C | 45 / 42 | 60 / 55 |

Since the baseline on the virtual gas module is about 102, the gas thresholds come to about 153 / 127 and 255 / 204.

### The outputs are worked out from the level
If you change the outputs from different places in the code, one day the level and the outputs will get out of step: the screen says TEHLIKE while the buzzer is silent. That is why a single function (`ciktilariSur`) looks at the level on every pass and sets all the outputs.

- **NORMAL:** green LED, silent.
- **UYARI:** yellow LED, one short beep a second (`simdi % 1000 < 150`).
- **TEHLIKE:** red LED, continuous sound.
- **Relay (fan):** depends only on the **gas** level. The fan runs until the gas is NORMAL; a flame on its own does not switch the fan on.

The screen answers the panel's "why" question: the second line shows each sensor's letter, like `G:N A:U I:N`.

### Non-blocking flow
The panel does not use `delay`. The measurement is refreshed every 100 ms and the screen every 250 ms; the rest of the time `loop` goes round and refreshes the outputs. That way the beep pattern, the screen and the measurement all work at the same time.

### Safety and limits
The relay only drives a 5 V LED; no circuit is ever built with mains voltage (220 V). This panel is for learning and does not replace a certified gas or fire detector. You must wait for the warm-up and measure the baseline in clean air.

### Summary
The panel finds a level from each sensor, makes the worst of the levels the overall state and works out all the outputs from that state. The relay is tied to the gas by a separate rule. Warm-up, baseline measurement and hysteresis reduce false alarms. The panel's limits (that it is for learning) must be stated clearly.
