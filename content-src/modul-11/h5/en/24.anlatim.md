### What you will learn in this lesson
- That the same collision can have two different results, and how to separate them with a condition
- How to use the difference between `this sprite` and `other sprite` in a collision event
- How to combine two conditions with `and`
- How to build the stomp test (falling speed + position)

### What you should know first
In lesson 6 you saw the collision event and `other sprite`. In lessons 21-22 you learnt that the y speed is positive downwards. The patrol logic from lesson 16 (an enemy that turns round at a wall) moves the enemy in this scene. Remember the `if ... else` block from lesson 9.

### One collision, two results
In a platform game there are two ways to touch an enemy: by falling on it from above (the enemy is beaten) or by walking into its side (the hero gets hurt). The engine's collision event does not tell them apart; it just says "the two overlapped". You tell them apart with a condition.

### Two tests
We recognise a hit from above by looking at two things:

```
Test 1: is the hero falling?     y speed > 10
Test 2: is the hero above?       kahraman y < enemy y - 4
```

A hero walking on the ground has a y speed of 0, so test 1 rules out a side hit. Test 2 rules out a hero that touches the enemy while falling past its side. Because y grows downwards, the y value of the sprite on top is **smaller**.

### Both together: and
If both tests are true, it is a stomp. The `and` in the logic block is only true if both sides are true:

```
if (y speed > 10) and (kahraman y < enemy y - 4) do:
  stomp
else:
  side hit
```

Example: the hero is falling at speed 120, y = 70; the enemy's y = 88. Test 1: 120 > 10 is true. Test 2: 70 < 84 is true. Result: a stomp.

Another example: the speed is 120 but the hero's y = 86. Test 2: 86 < 84 is false. Result: a side hit.

### Who is who?
In the `when a [Player] sprite hits a [Enemy] sprite` event, `this sprite` is the sprite of the first kind (the hero) and `other sprite` the sprite of the second kind (the enemy). To blow up the enemy you write `make other sprite explode`. If you blow up `this sprite`, the hero disappears.

### Reward and penalty in separate branches
The score and the bounce go in the `do` branch; the lives loss and going back to the start go in the `else` branch. If you put the lives loss outside the condition, you lose a life when you stomp too.

### Summary
The collision event tells you that two sprites touched; you work out how they touched with a condition. For a hit from above, the hero must be falling (y speed > 10) and a little above the enemy. You combine the two tests with `and`, and separate the stomp from the side hit with `if ... else`. `this sprite` points to the hero and `other sprite` to the enemy.
