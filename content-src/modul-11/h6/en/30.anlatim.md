### From an empty stage to a complete game
There is no ready-made game in this lesson. The stage is empty, and you make the decisions. But the path is clear: first the card, then four rounds.

| Round | Time | Job | When is it done? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 min | Player, key movement, score and lives | The player walks |
| 2 | 10 min | Something to collect, a danger, collision events | The score goes up, lives go down |
| 3 | 5 min | Winning rule | The game ends |
| 4 | 10 min | Sound, shakes, messages, adjusting | The game can be felt |

That makes 35 minutes in total. The order matters: adding polish to a game with no ending is a waste of time.

### The kind must be right
The Goals panel plays your game like a robot. It walks the player with the arrow keys, makes it collide with a sprite of the "Food", "Bonus", "Goal" or "Object" kind, and tries out the danger ("Enemy" or "Obstacle"). The panel does not check whether it likes your design; it looks at the **kinds** and the **events**.

That is why a sprite's kind must match the kind the collision event expects. If you make the sprite with the **Object** kind and the event expects **Food**, the event never runs. Even if the picture is the same, the event looks at the kind, not the picture.

### Adjusting with numbers
When you finish your game, play it yourself. If it is too easy, raise the speed or the target; if it is too hard, lower them. Work out the numbers:

- If you have 3 lives and each collision takes 1 life, you can collide at most 2 times; on the third, lives reach 0 and the game is lost.
- If a cherry appears every second and each cherry lives for 5 seconds, there are at most 5 cherries on the screen.
- If the target score is 5 and each cherry is worth 1 point, you must collect at least 5 cherries.

### Saving and the ↺ buttons
The **↺ Start over** button on the activity screen takes the activity back to the starter project; the blocks you made are gone. The ↺ on the stage only restarts the game. Do not mix the two up.

### Summary
To make your own game, fill in the design card first, then go through the skeleton, danger, ending and polish in that order. The Goals panel looks at kinds and events, so choose sprite kinds correctly. After finishing the game, adjust the numbers (speed, time, target score) by trying them. The two ↺ buttons do different jobs; with one of them you can lose your work.
