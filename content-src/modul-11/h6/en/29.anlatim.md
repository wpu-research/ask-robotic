### Bugs do not vanish; they are found
Every programmer makes mistakes; the difference is in how you look for them. The method has four steps:

| Step | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the symptom? | The score goes down when you catch a fruit |
| 2 | In which event does it happen? | When Player hits Food |
| 3 | Which block in that event is responsible? | `change score by [-1]` |
| 4 | Fix it, press ↺, test it | Change -1 to 1 |

Change only one thing at a time. If you change three things at once, you cannot know which one worked.

### Collision once, frame event every frame
The two kinds of event work very differently:

- The **collision event** runs **once**, at the moment two sprites touch. Even if the sprites stay on top of each other, the event does not repeat.
- The **every frame** event runs on every frame of the game. The game draws 60 frames a second, so the event runs 60 times a second.

This difference is the source of the third bug. A missed fruit at the bottom of the screen has `y > 116`. If the rule does not delete the fruit, the fruit stays there and takes a life every frame. Three lives go in 3 ÷ 60 = 0.05 seconds, that is 50 milliseconds. The game is over before the player can see what happened.

The fix is to make the rule happen only once: when a life is taken, the fruit is deleted too. A deleted fruit can no longer trigger the `y > 116` condition again.

### Working out the falling time
A fruit appears at y = -8 and counts as missed when `y > 116`. The distance to travel is 116 - (-8) = 124 pixels. A fruit with a speed of 62 pixels a second is missed in 124 ÷ 62 = 2 seconds. This calculation shows how much time a fruit gives the player: about 3.5 seconds if the speed is 35, and about 1.8 seconds if the speed is 70.

### Polish
The job is not finished just because the game's rules work. **Polish** means small extras that do not change the rules but make the game feel alive: a sound when you catch something, a screen shake when you miss, an explosion effect. Polish tells the player "something happened". Add polish only after you have fixed the bugs; polishing a broken game is pointless.

### Summary
Debugging means, in order, seeing the symptom, finding the event and the block, and fixing and testing. The collision event runs once and the `every frame` event 60 times a second; that is why forgetting to delete a sprite in an every-frame event leads to lives being lost very fast. Polish does not change the rules, but it makes the game clearer and more fun.
